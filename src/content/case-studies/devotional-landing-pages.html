01 / Context

Generic deep links broke the emotional promise of outreach.

Ahead of Pitru Paksha, one of Sri Mandir's highest revenue seasons, leadership challenged teams to unlock new conversion levers. Previously, devotees tapping a WhatsApp campaign link landed directly on a generic Puja listing screen (L2) or an app install prompt.

While functional for high intent users, dropping devotees straight into a catalogue stripped away sacred context, cultural storytelling, and personal recognition. Partnering with CRM Manager Siddharth, I owned the end to end narrative, UX architecture, visual design, and rapid HTML deployment for a curated intermediate layer (L1) before checkout.

Design question
How might we build a lightweight, culturally resonant landing layer between outreach and checkout that guides older and non technical devotees to the right ritual without slowing down high intent buyers?
02 / Discovery & Constraints

User survey signals and real world engineering guardrails.

Before designing screens, I partnered with our Product Manager to structure an intent survey sent to roughly 200 past Pitru Paksha devotees. While other fields saw drop offs, sacred location preference achieved nearly a 100% response rate, proving that where an ancestral ritual happens matters deeply to devotees.

Data privacy and attribute guardrail

Although we wanted to greet users by first name, database records risked pulling a deceased ancestor's name from past ritual logs. To prevent a sensitive error, I standardised greetings to Namaste, Sri Mandir Bhakt and anchored personalisation strictly on the user's last booked Puja attribute.

One day turnaround via AI workflow

With zero dedicated frontend bandwidth and strict MoEngage tracking requirements, I designed layouts in Figma, generated base code via Figma Make, converted assets to self contained Base64 HTML using Claude and GPT, and injected MoEngage click tracking functions directly into every CTA.

Accessible hierarchy for mixed ages

Because many devotees are senior citizens, pages eliminated top navigation bars, maintained familiar Sri Mandir Puja card patterns, and placed single focused actions below clear cultural context.

03 / Experiment 1: Pitru Paksha

Location led storytelling paired with past ritual recall.

For the opening of Pitru Paksha, I structured the page around four deliberate layers: personal recall of last year's ritual, an auspicious date banner, Pujas grouped by sacred city (Gaya, Kashi, Haridwar, Gokarna) using mythological angles aligned with marketing, and a closing scripture backed trust anchor.

Where a single Puja was conducted across two ghats (such as Gaya and Haridwar), I contextualised each card to display only the matching city's ghat and distinct thumbnail so devotees browsing by location experienced zero confusion before reaching the full booking screen. 04 / Experiment 2: Mahalaya Amavasya

Visual mood shift and social proof badges for the finale.

Insights from Pitru Paksha showed markedly stronger engagement among Hindi speaking devotees, prompting a focused Hindi rollout for Mahalaya Amavasya. Because Mahalaya marks the solemn culmination of Pitru Paksha, I shifted from bright daylight tones to warm sunset and night browns aligned with brand communications.

Using booking data from the opening campaign, I introduced social proof tags on Gaya and Haridwar, refined three visual colour directions with internal stakeholder feedback (selecting Variation 1), and ran a controlled split testing personalisation against a date significance card. 05 / Experiment 3: Navratri

Pivoting from geography to sacred day significance.

Unlike Pitru Paksha, Navratri devotion centres on specific lunar days rather than pilgrimage cities, and Puja inventory was available for three key milestones: Day 1 (Pratipada), Day 7 (Maha Saptami), and Days 8 & 9 (Ashtami and Navami).

I restructured the narrative to educate devotees on why these specific days carry peak spiritual weight, added interactive quick jump tabs so returning devotees could skip straight to their preferred day, and standardised brand trust by embedding the Sri Mandir logo and adaptive website footer across every subsequent page. As each festival day passed, I rapidly pruned expired Pujas from the live MoEngage HTML to maintain trust. Explore the live Navratri campaign page.

06 / Experiments 4 & 5: Kali Chaudas & Rama Ekadashi

UI evolution: from app familiar cards to illustrated mythological chapters.

Once Performance Marketing began routing cold paid traffic to these pages, neither past user attributes nor prime temple locations were available. Up through Navratri, Puja cards intentionally mirrored the Sri Mandir app UI to preserve familiarity for returning users. With Kali Chaudas and Rama Ekadashi, I shifted focus to evolving the visual language and interaction design.

Kali Chaudas: thematic narrative and golden iconography

Built on a deep midnight blue palette with custom golden framed chapter thumbnails and a vertical dotted timeline connector. The visual direction balanced Maa Kali's fierce protective energy within brand guidelines while pairing a three part origin story with three curated Pujas (15.38% CTR across 195 paid views).

Rama Ekadashi: illustrated storybook and bespoke Puja cards

With stronger control over the AI assisted HTML build, I completely redesigned both the storytelling and booking UI to read like an illustrated mythological book. Large vertical story cards arranged in a horizontal scroll gave artwork room to breathe as sequential chapters, while Puja cards were reskinned in matching dark gold gradients to maintain visual cohesion (~15% CTR and 4x higher L2 purchase rate than direct Meta ads).

Responsive brand footer system

Standardised from Navratri onwards across all pages, the Sri Mandir website footer adapted seamlessly between desktop and mobile viewports, requiring only a background colour token swap to match each festival's theme.

Cohort analysis across 1,41,931 users confirmed that curated landing pages act as a powerful intent filter: fewer users clicked through aimlessly to L2, yet buyer conversion among L2 landers jumped from 25.8% to 31.1% (+13.6% lift among repeat devotees).
Following a 13.6% repeat buyer lift across 1,41,931 users, the core Product team adopted this pattern, and I am now designing native in app contextual landing flows ahead of key product journeys.
08 / Takeaways

What this experiment series taught me.

Context converts existing intent

Explaining why a ritual or tithi matters before showing prices removes decision fatigue and doubles package commitment.

Match the narrative hook to audience warmth

Returning devotees respond strongest to personal recall and location familiarity, whereas cold paid audiences need cultural storytelling and high visual craft to build trust.

AI accelerated execution compounds learning velocity

Combining Figma design systems with AI assisted Base64 HTML and MoEngage instrumentation compressed multi day engineering cycles into 24 hour launches, enabling five iterative experiments in a single festival season.
